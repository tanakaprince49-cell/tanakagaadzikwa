# Official boilerplate — Tanaka Gaadzikwa

**Copy these verbatim. Do not rewrite them per profile.**

Identical wording across profiles is what lets Google merge separate pages into one
person. If your LinkedIn says "teen techpreneur from Zim" and your Crunchbase says
"young software builder", Google treats them as possibly different people and builds
no panel. Same words, everywhere.
